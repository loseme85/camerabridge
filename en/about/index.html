Why I built this

I used to look after VIP clients at an official Leica Store, and later managed a private premium camera shop. The hardest part of the job was always buying. Someone brings in a lens, and you have to name a price on the spot, knowing that version, condition and what comes in the box can change its value several times over. Before I had the experience, it made me anxious every time, and it was even harder for colleagues who weren't quick with numbers.

I work in a different field now, but that frustration stayed with me, so I built Camera Bridge. It collects listings from shops in many countries and groups them precisely by model, version and generation. I hope it takes some of the stress out of pricing, whether you're buying stock, selling it, or buying a camera for yourself.

Camera Bridge is an independent, one-person service with no connection to Leica Camera AG.

What Camera Bridge is

Camera Bridge is a search service that collects used Leica listings and prices published by camera shops in several countries. We do not sell items or broker transactions; purchase, payment, shipping and returns are always between you and the shop.

Affiliate disclosure

Some shop links (for example eBay) may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, Camera Bridge may earn a commission from the shop at no extra cost to you. Commissions do not affect the order of results or the prices shown.
