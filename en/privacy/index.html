Privacy policy

thehingemeta ("we") operates Camera Bridge (camerabridge.io). Camera Bridge works without an account, and we process only the minimal information below. This policy is written to meet Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and the EU/UK GDPR.

What we process, why, and on what basis

Feedback formYour message, email (optional), and a sender tag (a daily-rotating hash of your IP address; the IP itself is not stored) — to read and reply to feedback and to block spam (5 per day). Basis: your consent by sending it, and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.
Community board (Google sign-in)A member ID (a one-way hash of your Google account ID), your nickname, the text of your posts and comments (public — anyone can read them), for reports a sender tag (a daily-rotating hash of your IP address) and the report text, the listings you save (link plus title and price at the time you saved), your listing alerts (model, minimum grade, maximum delivered total and destination country, plus the listings already emailed), and — only if you turn on price alerts — your Google account email — to recognise you and keep you signed in, run the board, show your saved listings, email you when a saved listing drops in price or sells or when a new listing matches your alert, block spam (5 posts and 30 comments per day) and handle reports. Your Google name and photo are not stored; your email is stored only while alerts are on. Basis: your consent by signing in and posting, and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.
Shop (dealer) accountsUsername, shop name and password (stored only as a one-way hash) — to sign in to the dealer view. Basis: contract with the shop.
Usage logCountry code of the connection, interface language and currency, referring page (domain and path, without the query string), first page and pages viewed on the site, an identifier to count the same connection once per day (a hash of the IP address that changes daily; the IP itself is not stored), mobile or not, time on page, searches, chosen models, result counts, clicks to shops, a visit ID per browser tab, and a random browser identifier — to improve the service and measure return visits, first visits and latest visits. The browser identifier is generated independently of IP addresses and accounts, and is not linked to sign-in information. No IP address itself, email or cookie is kept. Basis: legitimate interest in running and improving the service.
AnalyticsAggregated visit statistics from Vercel Web Analytics (cookieless, does not identify you).

How long we keep it

  • Feedback: 1 year from receipt (or from the last message if we keep corresponding)
  • Shop accounts: until the account is closed
  • Board posts and comments: until you delete them. Deleted posts and posts taken down by the operator are kept privately for 30 days to handle reports and disputes, then deleted. Member ID, nickname, saved listings and listing alerts: until you ask us to delete your account (a saved listing or alert is deleted as soon as you remove it). Alert email: until you turn alerts off (via the link in any alert email or on your saved list), then deleted immediately
  • Usage log: 1 year from the day it was recorded
  • After that, data is deleted without delay.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, except where required by law.

Processors and international transfers

Vercel Inc. (USA)Hosting, private storage of feedback, board posts, shop accounts and usage log (Vercel Blob), analytics
Telegram FZ-LLC (UAE)Forwards new feedback, board posts and reports to the operator as a notification
Resend Inc. (USA)Sends price-alert emails (recipient address and message)
Google LLC (USA)Verifies Google sign-in for the board. To show the sign-in button we load Google's script, which may set Google's own cookies under Google's privacy policy

Data is transferred over the internet when you use the service. Transfers to the US rely on Vercel's standard contractual clauses / data processing terms.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your information, object to processing, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing thehingemeta@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority. Usage-log entries are not linked to anything that identifies you, so we may not be able to single out a particular visit.

Cookies and browser storage

We set a sign-in cookie only when a shop signs in (cb_dealer) or when you sign in to the board with Google (cb_member, 90 days); both are strictly necessary to keep you signed in. Regular visitors get no cookies. We use your browser's local storage to remember the language, currency and shipping country you chose; you can clear it in your browser settings. We also store a random browser identifier (cb_browser) for return-visit statistics for up to one year from creation. Clearing storage or using another browser or device starts a separate history. Turn off usage logging to delete this identifier and stop logging this browser, or turn it back on.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Stored data is kept in private storage that only the operator can access, and passwords are stored only as hashes.

Children

Camera Bridge is not directed to children under 14 (under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their information.
