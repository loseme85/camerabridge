## Candidate Under Review

### A. later built-in-hood / commonly dealer-labeled `2세대 캐나다`

## Literature / Reference Basis

Leica Wiki documents `75mm f/1.4 Summilux-M` as a real line with internal progression:

- order numbers `11814`, `11815` (built-in hood), `11810`
- production era `1980-2007`
- after `1982`, a built-in-hood version `11815`
- Canada / Germany inscription history

This means the underlying version distinction is real.  
The literature does **not** literally say that the canonical subtype name is `2세대 캐나다`, but it does support the existence of a later built-in-hood progression that local dealer language may be approximating.

## Mechanical / Optical Distinction

### Mechanical distinction

Real and meaningful.

The built-in-hood progression is a genuine version change, not just finish, packaging, or condition.

### Optical distinction

Weak as a separate optical family.

This is best understood as an internal version/build progression inside one broad `Summilux 75` line, not as a wholly separate Leica 75 product family.

## Search-Intent Separation

### Conceptual search intent

Moderate.

If a seller or user explicitly writes `2세대 캐나다`, they are clearly pointing at a narrower subgroup than broad `Summilux 75`.

### Admin-normalization usefulness

Moderate to strong.

Unlike some literature-real but title-invisible subtypes, this cluster shows repeated, almost templated local wording. That gives it practical admin value even if the wording is imperfect as pure canonical truth.

But `Germany` is more ambiguous as a canonical row name because it mixes:

- country marking

Whereas `2세대 캐나다` is less clean as Leica literature language, but more operationally stable as one repeated dealer cluster.

## Broad Core Relationship

Current broad family core:

- `Leica Summilux-M 75mm f/1.4`

Question:

Does a separate `hold` row create real value, or just make taxonomy prettier?

Answer:

It creates real value **if** we treat it as an operational admin-normalization row rather than as perfect canonical truth.

Why:

- broad `Summilux 75` should remain the family anchor
- `2세대 캐나다` appears often enough to deserve explicit capture
- a `hold` row is the right level because the wording is useful but not ideal as a final market taxonomy name

## Final Recommendation

### Verdict

### Why

The local cluster is:

- repeated
- price-coherent
- operationally useful
- tied to a real underlying version progression

It is still not clean enough for `core`, but it is strong enough for an explicit `hold` row.
